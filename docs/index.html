Guide

What everything on this page means, in plain words.

Verdict
The bot's call on a coin for the next ~7 days: STRONG BUY, BUY, HOLD, SELL or STRONG SELL. STRONG means the trusted strategies all agree.
Score
From -1 (very bearish) to +1 (very bullish). It combines the strategies that worked best recently (and the AI model, when it has passed its exam).
Chance up
The AI model's estimate that the price is higher in 7 days. Shows '-' when the model is switched off because it couldn't beat a coin flip on its last exam.
Risk level
From 20,000 simulated futures based on the coin's own past swings: LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH or EXTREME, by the chance of falling 25%+ within 30 days.
Strategies
Classic trading rules (trend following, momentum, MACD, Turtle breakout, ...). Only the ones that made money in recent months get a vote.
Practice trade / call
The bot's top picks that weren't strong enough to alert you. Tracked like real ones so it can learn from them. No alerts are sent for these.
Paper trading
Every trade here is pretend - nothing is bought. It's how the bot proves itself before you risk real money.
After costs
Day-trade results subtract 0.25% per trade for Binance fees and slippage.
Beat BTC
The honest test. A buy call beats Bitcoin if the coin rose more than Bitcoin did; a sell call beats Bitcoin if the coin fell more. Being 'right' just because the whole market moved doesn't count as skill.
Exam score (AUC)
How well the model ranks winners above losers on recent data it never saw. 0.50 = coin flip. Below 0.53 the model switches itself off.
Safety switch
If the last ~25 real day-trade signals lost money on average, new signals pause until a new training finds an edge again.
Market-weather filter
If training finds that buys made while Bitcoin trends down lost money, buy signals pause during Bitcoin downtrends.
Hype stage
EARLY = buzz before the price moved. BUILDING = both rising. LATE = already pumped. FADING = people talk about it less. MILD = no real hype.

Everything here is paper trading. Treat calls as 'worth a look', never 'buy now'. Not financial advice.
